Research groups

Four groups, one axis of scale

Our groups are not separate departments. They are four positions on a single continuum — the same disease read as a molecule, as a patient record, as a prediction, and as a place.

Individual Patients & records

Computational Biology

Multi-omics integration, bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, and structural modelling to locate the mechanisms and biomarkers behind complex disease.

Public Health & Informatics

Survey analytics, clinical registries and digital health evaluation, quantifying disease burden and health inequity across Bangladesh and South Asia.

Bio-AI

Risk prediction, medical image analysis and interpretable machine learning built for clinical settings where transparency matters as much as accuracy.

Geospatial Health

How we work

A research centre that is also a school

CHIRAL is volunteer-run and charges nothing. What we offer instead is supervision, method, and the expectation that a first-year student can produce work that survives peer review.

Students lead

Undergraduates take first authorship and run their own analyses under one-to-one supervision.

No fees, no stipends

Entirely voluntary in both directions. We measure ourselves in publications and careers, not revenue.

Disciplines mix

Computer scientists, molecular biologists, clinicians, statisticians and geographers on the same projects.

Open by default

Pipelines, teaching material and code are published openly so the capacity stays in the region.
